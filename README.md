# Aegis — Cybersecurity and Ethical Hacking Club
### *Your Data. Your Right. Stay Vigilant.*
**RV Institute of Technology and Management**  
**Department of Information Science and Engineering**

Aegis started with a simple goal: give students a place to actually practice security skills hands-on, rather than just reading about them. Since its inauguration in December 2022, the club has grown into a community of over 85 members, built around workshops, hands-on training sessions, and Capture the Flag challenges that push members to apply what they learn rather than just study it.

The club runs on a simple belief: understanding how systems break is the fastest way to learn how to defend them — and that responsibility comes with the territory. Every session, from networking fundamentals to more advanced offensive techniques, is taught with that ethical grounding front and center.

Cybersecurity is Aegis's core identity, but members aren't limited to security work in isolation — many projects integrate security thinking directly into other technical domains, whether that's securing an AI/ML pipeline, hardening a web or app project, or working through Cloud/DevOps with a security-first mindset.

Much of this hands-on work happens in Aegis's own dedicated Cybersecurity Lab — a space set up specifically for members to practice rather than just watch. Through a partnership with ReInfosec Lab, the club also offers members structured courses that go deeper into specific security topics than a single workshop session allows.

Aegis also runs an annual five-day workshop, a different cybersecurity topic explored in depth each year, alongside its regular inductions and member drives that bring in new students every academic cycle.

## Leadership

Aegis is guided by **Niharika P. Kumar**, Associate Professor in the Department of Information Science and Engineering, who has served as the club's Faculty Coordinator.

The club is currently led by **Yashash Mathur** (Information Science and Engineering), as Club Head, supported by a core team of student leads who run the club's day-to-day activities, workshops, and technical direction. As with most student-run clubs, this core team changes as senior members graduate — leadership here reflects the current academic year.

## Segments

Aegis organizes its project work into segments, reflecting both the depth of the club's core focus and the range of domains its members build in.

**Cybersecurity**
- Exploitation & Ethical Hacking
- Web Application Security
- Network Security
- Cryptography
- Reverse Engineering
- Malware Analysis
- Digital Forensics & Incident Response
- Open Source Intelligence (OSINT)
- Threat Hunting & Vulnerability Assessment
- Cloud Security
- Hardware & Embedded Security
- Privacy & Risk

**Other Domains**
- AI/ML & Data Science
- Web Development
- App Development
- Cloud/DevOps

Each repository under this organization is tagged with its segment, making it possible to browse or filter projects by area of focus.
